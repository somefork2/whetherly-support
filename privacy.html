Privacy Policy

Updated 20 November 2026 · effective from Whetherly 1.1 · applies to the Whetherly app for iPhone

Short version: Your name, birth details, the people you add and your dream journal stay on your device. The app collects usage statistics and crash reports to help us improve it. There are no accounts and no ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your name, birth date, time and place, the people you add for compatibility, your dream journal entries and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to our analytics provider.

Usage statistics and crash reports

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to understand how the app is used. It receives:

  • Actions in the app, such as screens opened, onboarding steps completed, features used, and the plan chosen on the subscription screen.
  • General preferences and counts, such as app language, the topics you pick as interests, whether a birth time was provided, whether notifications are on, and how many people or dreams are saved, shown as a range.
  • Subscription activity, such as plan, price, currency, and whether a purchase or trial started, was cancelled or failed.
  • Technical data, such as an app instance identifier generated by Firebase, device model, iOS and app version, App Store country, approximate location derived from your IP address, and crash and freeze reports.

This data is not used for advertising. The app does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. Learn more about privacy and security in Firebase.

Calculations

Charts, forecasts, calendars and dream readings are calculated entirely on your device. Sleep sounds are synthesised on the device.

City search

When you search for your birthplace, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps (MapKit) to find the city, its coordinates and time zone. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Only the search text is sent, not your name or other details.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Notifications

Morning forecasts and moon/retrograde reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app (Profile) or in iPhone Settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Profile → Erase all my data deletes everything the app stored on your device. Deleting the app also removes all its data from your device. To ask about or delete usage statistics linked to your app instance, write to support.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
