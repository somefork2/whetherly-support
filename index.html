Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the calculations?
Planet positions are computed on your device from NASA JPL DE440 ephemeris data, accurate to a fraction of an arc-minute. Houses use Placidus or whole signs (switch in Profile).

I don't know my birth time.
Choose “I don't know my exact time”. Planets in signs stay exact; rising sign and houses are approximate. You can add the time later in Profile → Edit birth details.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
Open Profile → Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid renewal.

How do I restore purchases?
Profile → Restore purchases, or the same button on the Plus screen.

How do I change the language?
Profile → Language. 11 languages are available.

How do I delete my data?
Everything is stored only on your device. Profile → Erase all my data removes it; deleting the app does too.

Is this scientific advice?
No. Whetherly is a tool for self-reflection and inspiration and is not medical, legal or financial advice.
